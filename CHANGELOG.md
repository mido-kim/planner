# Changelog

Each version lists what changed for you as a user. The version number also
appears at the bottom of Settings.

## 0.11.1 (2026-10-27)

- Sticking stickers is easier on a phone:
  - Tap a sticker in the tray (it gets a pink ring), then tap the calendar
    where it goes. Taps on the calendar stick it rather than opening things,
    and swipes still move the dates meanwhile. Tap the sticker again to
    cancel. On a phone the tray goes back to one row so the days show.
  - A sticker dragged with a finger rides just above it, so you can see where
    it lands, and lands where it shows.
  - Small wobbles of a finger count as a tap, not a drag or a swipe.
  - Stuck stickers are easier to hit with a finger.
- Settings → Calendar → Notes on the calendar: show the notes of events,
  tasks and deadlines, each on or off. Tasks and deadlines show theirs on one
  line under the name, on the day, week and 2-week views. Events stay on;
  tasks and deadlines start off.

## 0.11.0 (2026-10-27)

- 127 letter stickers come with the app, cut from three sticker sheets, each
  A to Z:
  - Hearts: 25 pink candy hearts (the sheet has no X).
  - Patches: 76 patch letters, several of most letters (floral, gingham,
    leopard, chrome, crowned …).
  - Beads: 26 white letter beads.
- The sticker tray has categories along its bottom, like the emoji keyboard:
  Mine (the glitter star and the stickers you make), Hearts, Patches, Beads.
- The tray shows one row of stickers (five at a time, sideways for more).
  Swipe it up (or tap its arrow) for rows of them, and down for one row
  again. In the rows, hold a sticker a moment before dragging it out (a quick
  swipe scrolls).
- While a sticker is dragged out of the tray, the tray fades, so you can drop
  it on the days behind it.

## 0.10.1 (2026-10-27)

- Sticker sizes are smaller: Large is what Medium was, Medium what Small was,
  and Small smaller again.
- Tap a stuck sticker to pick it, as in PowerPoint: it gets a dashed outline
  and a round handle above it. Drag the handle to turn the sticker, in steps
  of 15°. A small bar under it has S, M, L and Remove (Delete or Backspace
  removes it on a PC too). Tap anywhere else to let go.
- A picked sticker moves at once when dragged with a finger, without holding
  first. Turned stickers stay turned when moved.

## 0.10.0 (2026-10-27)

- An event's notes show inside its box on the day, week and 2-week views, in
  the room left below the time: whole lines, the last one faded when there's
  more. Month view items have no room for them.
- Stickers. The sticker button (beside the agenda button on a PC, beside the
  arrows on a phone) opens the sticker tray.
  - **Add** makes a sticker from a picture: the background around it (the
    color along its edges) turns see-through and it's cut to fit. You see it
    small, medium and large before adding it, and can keep the background
    instead. Stickers sync to your other devices.
  - Drag a sticker from the tray onto the calendar. Pick S, M or L in the
    tray first.
  - On the day, week and 2-week views, a sticker dropped on an event goes
    with that event (it moves when the event moves). On a repeating event
    you're asked: just that one, or that one and all following. Dropped
    anywhere else, it stays at that day and time.
  - Timeline stickers grow and shrink with the hours, so they keep the same
    place beside your events in all three views.
  - Month view stickers stay on their day there, and only show in the month
    view.
  - Drag a stuck sticker to move it (on a phone, hold it first). Tap it to
    make it small, medium or large, or to remove it.
  - The glitter star comes with the app.
- Backups include stickers and where they're stuck.

## 0.9.0 (2026-10-21)

- Light is now called **Gelato Days**, after the palette its colors came
  from, and Dark is **Gelato Nights**. They look the same as before.
- Fifteen pastel themes, in their own "Pastel" group under Settings →
  Appearance → Theme: Lazy Days, Lilac Dreamer, Summer Energy, Feeling Fall,
  Sweet Sunset, Sophisticated City, In The Sunshine, Garden Party, Periwinkle
  Cloud, Enchanting Forest, Toned Down, Smoothie Bar, Blues, Cafe Mocha and
  Pastel Coffee Shop. Each takes its background tint, text and accent from
  its palette. Label colors stay distinct (blue is still blue), but take on
  the palette's softness and use its own color wherever one is close.
- Swiping on a phone is smoother:
  - The dates move as layers while your finger drags them.
  - When you let go, they glide on at your finger's speed instead of
    stopping and starting again.
  - The settle is a touch calmer.
  - The agenda waits until the dates have settled before it redraws.
  The mouse wheel and buttons on a PC move the same as before.

## 0.8.1 (2026-10-21)

- Text on the calendar is 1px larger: event names and times, all-day bars,
  deadline and task names, month chips, hour numbers and weekday names. The
  big date numbers stay as they were.
- Event boxes leave a little more room above their text. A 2-week box too
  short for a line of text shows its name in the middle instead, so the
  letters aren't cut off.
- Dragging to make something now works across days:
  - On the week and 2-week timelines, drag from one day into another (in the
    2-week view, on into the next week too) to select from that time to that
    time. The menu offers an event for exactly that time, or an all-day
    event on those days.
  - In the 2-week view's all-day rows, a drag can go on into the other week.
    Before, it jumped back to the same weekday of the first week.
  - Wandering onto the hour labels, a date heading or past the last week
    picks the nearest day instead of stopping.
- Days being selected or dropped on are pink on weekends and today too.
  Before, weekends kept their gray in the month and 2-week views, and the
  day under the mouse in the month view showed its hover gray.

## 0.8.0 (2026-10-21)

- Working hours can be several stretches of the day, each shaded on its
  own: Settings → Calendar → Working hours, with "Add hours" and a remove
  button on each row. It starts at 10 AM–12 PM, 1–6 PM and 7–9 PM on every
  device.
- Seven more themes to switch between (Settings → Appearance → Theme), made
  from well-loved color palettes. Light ones: Latte, Rosé Dawn and Nord
  Snow. Dark ones: Mocha, Rosé Pine, Everforest and Tokyo Night. Light and
  Dark stay as they were.
- The theme maker with its sliders is gone. Themes made with it still show
  in the list under "Made earlier".

## 0.7.1 (2026-10-20)

- "No symbol" is a choice in every symbol picker (labels and events), for
  events that don't need checking off, like trips across several days.
- The 2-week view writes each event's start time in its box, after its name
  (on the next line when the name fills the first).
- The hour column on the left shows just numbers: 7, 8 … 12, 1.
- Tasks and deadlines can turn into each other: "Turn into a deadline" in a
  task, "Turn into a task" in a deadline. Name, notes, label, color, date,
  time and done state come along (a task without a date becomes due today).
  For a repeating deadline only that date turns into a task. Undo is offered.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-20)

- Deadlines can be checked off like tasks: tap the flag. It fills in, the
  name is crossed out, and the deadline leaves the agenda (until "Show past
  or completed").
- Events have a little symbol in front of their name: tap it to mark the
  event done (went, attended, finished). It's an outline until then and
  fills in after. Symbols: check box (the default), circle, heart, star,
  sparkle, speech bubble, book, light bulb, beaker, lightning, music note,
  coffee, leaf, dumbbell, smile and bookmark.
- A label can have a symbol for all its events (Labels → the pencil, or when
  adding one), and an event can have its own (event editor → Symbol; "Same
  as label" follows its label).
- A repeating event or deadline is checked off one date at a time, and those
  dates go along when the series is moved, split or one date is changed.
- Long dialogs scroll instead of squeezing their rows together.

## 0.6.2 (2026-10-20)

- Moving to other dates glides like scrolling a page: the old dates move out
  while the new ones come in right behind them and settle into place with a
  soft spring, instead of fading in. In the day and week views the hour
  labels stay put while the days slide sideways; in the month view the
  weekday row stays put and a week up or down moves by exactly one row. The
  same for the wheel, the arrow buttons and keys, and swipes (the days follow
  your finger and carry on from where you let go).
- Settings → Appearance → Moving between dates: Slide and settle, or Fade.
  It slides even when the device asks apps for less motion (Windows with
  animation effects off does); pick Fade for the calmer one.

## 0.6.1 (2026-10-20)

- The agenda only shows what's still ahead: events that are over (earlier
  days, and earlier today once they've ended) and done tasks are left out.
  "Show N past or completed" at the bottom brings them back. Overdue tasks
  still show.
- Mouse wheel on a PC, like swiping on the phone: over the calendar, tilt the
  wheel (or hold Shift and roll) to go to the next or previous day or week;
  roll it up or down in the 2-week and month views to move a week. One step
  per notch, with a short pause between steps so it doesn't race ahead.
- The Today button is a house icon now.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-20)

- Themes: Settings → Appearance → Theme picks System (light or dark as your
  phone or PC is set, as before), Light, Dark, or a theme you made.
- Theme maker (Settings → Make a theme…): hue, saturation and lightness
  sliders for each part (background, panels, buttons, lines, the three text
  shades, overdue red, the accent and its text, switches, links, the now line,
  and each item color with its text), plus how strong item fills, the today
  highlight and the working-hours shade are. The app changes as you slide.
  Give it a name to save it to your themes on all your devices; each device
  keeps its own choice. Without a name it's used on that device only.
- Saved themes are part of backups.

## 0.5.7 (2026-10-20)

- New fonts: Korean letters are in 느좋체 everywhere, and all English letters
  and numbers are in Instrument Serif, the names of events, deadlines and
  tasks included (they were Arial). Instrument Serif has no bold, so English
  names are now regular weight; Korean names keep their bold.
- The Korean font (about 0.9 MB per weight) is saved for offline use in the
  background, once.

## 0.5.6 (2026-10-17)

- Task and deadline names are back on one line: a long name is cut and faded
  at the end of its own day instead of running on over the next day or
  wrapping (that put text on top of other events, especially on a phone). The
  same in the month view.
- Task and deadline names that would land on top of each other, or on an
  event's title, now move a little up or down from their time until each one
  is readable, also when both have a time (before, only those without a time
  moved). Names never cover each other; in a very crowded spot (the 2-week
  view on a phone) a name may still touch an event's title rather than move
  hours away from its time.

## 0.5.5 (2026-10-15)

- Tasks and deadlines have no background any more, on the calendar and in the
  agenda: just the round tick box or flag, and the name in its color. Event
  cards in the agenda keep their pastel.
- Deadline names may run on over the next day too. A task or deadline name
  only does so when that spot is free on the next day; otherwise it wraps onto
  more lines in its own day (in the month view it's cut and faded).
- Tasks and deadlines without a time now sit around midnight (11:59 PM –
  12 AM) instead of at the very end of the day (2 AM). One that would land on
  another task or deadline moves a little up or down so their names don't
  overlap.
- Korean names break between words, not in the middle of one.

## 0.5.4 (2026-10-15)

- Smoother swipes: the calendar follows your finger, and when you let go past
  about a quarter of the way (or flick) it glides out while the next dates
  glide in; a short drag springs back. With Reduce Motion on (iPhone Settings →
  Accessibility → Motion) the dates fade instead of sliding.
- Tasks on the calendar: a small round tick box instead of a square, no
  outline, and the title in the task's color. The agenda uses the same round
  tick box.
- In the week, 2-week and month views, a task's title may run on over the
  next day instead of being cut at its own day's edge.

## 0.5.3 (2026-10-15)

- The Day and Week views now fit the whole day on the screen, so the timeline
  no longer scrolls up and down (and a sideways swipe can't nudge it). How tall
  an hour is follows the space there is; on a squeezed day only every other
  hour is labelled. To give each hour more room, show fewer hours (Settings →
  Day starts at / Day ends at). The Hour height setting is gone.

## 0.5.2 (2026-10-15)

- Swipe to move through dates on a phone: left or right in the Day and Week
  views for the next or previous day or week; up or down in the 2-week and
  Month views to move a week later or earlier. The month's name follows the
  month that fills most of the screen, and the arrows still go a month at a
  time. New dates slide in from the side they came from.

## 0.5.1 (2026-10-15)

- New font: Instrument Serif for the app itself (the dates at the top, view
  buttons, day numbers, hours, times, headings and buttons). Names of events,
  deadlines and tasks stay in Arial for now. Korean text keeps its usual font.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-15)

- Working hours (10 AM – 9 PM, Monday to Friday) are very lightly shaded on
  the timelines, except on Korean public holidays. Change the hours or turn
  the shading off in Settings.
- Korean public holidays from 2026 to 2031 are built in, with substitute
  holidays, 노동절, 제헌절 and election days. Their dates show in red, with the
  holiday's name when you point at them.
- The day now runs until 2 AM the next morning (Settings → Day ends at). Things
  after midnight show at the bottom of the evening before.
- Deadlines and tasks can lose their time: tap the × next to the time. A
  deadline without a time is due any time that day and shows at the day's end.
- Text that doesn't fit is cut off and fades out toward the end, instead of
  ending in "…".
- Drag events, deadlines and tasks to another time or day (on a phone, hold
  one for a moment first). Repeating events ask whether to move just that one,
  the following ones or all. Undo is offered after each move.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-14)

- New "Gelato Days" colors: soft pastel pink, green, yellow, blue, purple and
  peach (plus matching mint, coral and stone) for events, labels and the app
  itself, with pink as the main color. Your items keep their colors, in the new
  shades.
- The task list is now the Agenda: every event, deadline and task in the
  dates on screen with its full details (time, label, how it repeats,
  tentative, notes), plus overdue tasks and tasks with no date.
- On a PC the agenda can sit at the right or at the bottom (button in its
  header), can be resized by dragging its edge (double-click to reset), and
  hidden with × or the agenda button at the top.
- On a phone, the Day, Week and 2-week views show the calendar in the top half
  and the agenda in the bottom half. Drag the bar between them to change the
  split; the agenda button hides it. Month keeps the whole screen, with the
  agenda as a sheet.
- The 2-week view shows two short timelines (one per week), so items sit at
  their times and both weeks fit on screen. Click or drag on them to create.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-14)

- Deadlines: a single date and time, drawn as a line across the day with its
  title, and as a flagged chip in the 2-week and month grids. They can repeat.
- New buttons at the top for a new event, deadline or task. Clicking or
  dragging on the calendar now asks which of the three to make.
- On a phone, hold a finger on the timeline for a moment, then drag to pick a
  time range. A normal swipe still scrolls; a tap still picks an hour.
- Labels: give events, deadlines and tasks a label (Settings → Labels, or
  “Edit labels” in any editor). A label sets the item's color; any item can
  still pick its own color. Retire a label to hide it from the picker (its
  items keep it), restore it later, or delete it together with its items
  (with Undo). Labels sync and are part of backups.
- Tasks with a due date show on the calendar: at their time, or at the end of
  the day when they have no time. Tick them off right there; click to edit.
- The 2-week view is now a grid of two tall week rows, like the month view.
- All-day and multi-day events stretch across their days in the all-day row
  and in the 2-week and month grids, as a block, a line with arrowheads at both
  ends, or a plain line (pick in the event editor). Events of 24 hours or more
  move to the all-day row.
- The app's font is now Arial (with Apple SD Gothic Neo / Malgun Gothic for
  Korean).
- Event boxes no longer have a colored bar on the left.
- Short event boxes show the title first; the time only uses the room left.
- Hour height: 30 px is the new default (also offered in Settings next to 40,
  52 and 68). Devices still on the old default move to 30.

## 0.2.1 (2026-10-14)

  `&view=day|week|2week|month` or `&show=tasks|event|new-event|delete|task|settings`

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-14)

- Tentative events: turn on "Tentative" in the event editor. They're drawn with
  a light tint and a dashed outline in every view, and export to `.ics` as
  tentative.
- The version number is shown in Settings.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-14)

First version.

- Day, Week, 2 Weeks and Month views. In the first three, events sit on a
  timeline and are as tall as they are long; overlapping events share the
  column. Visible hours are adjustable.
- Repeating events (daily, weekly on chosen days, weekdays, monthly by date or
  weekday, yearly, every N…), ending never, on a date or after N times.
- Edit or delete this event / this and following / all events, with Undo.
- Tasks panel for the dates on screen, plus overdue and undated tasks.
- Works offline. Optional Firebase sync with email sign-in.
- JSON backup and restore, `.ics` export, backup reminder.
- Light and dark mode, installable on iPhone and PC.
